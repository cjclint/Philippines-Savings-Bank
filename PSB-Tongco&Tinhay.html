Background & History

Philippine Savings Bank (PSBank or PSB) was established on June 30, 1959, initially to primarily engage in savings and mortgage banking in the Philippines. The bank began its operations in Manila and has since grown to become the second-largest savings bank in the Philippines.

A significant milestone in PSBank's history came in 1983 when Metropolitan Bank and Trust Company (Metrobank) acquired a majority stake in the bank. This acquisition made PSBank a subsidiary of the Metrobank Group, one of the largest financial conglomerates in the Philippines. In 2004, Metrobank further increased its shareholdings to approximately 76%.

The bank reached important operational milestones in the early 1990s. In 1991, PSBank was authorized to perform trust functions, expanding its service offerings. In 1995, the bank was granted a quasi-banking license, further enhancing its capabilities in the financial sector.

Throughout its history, PSBank has focused primarily on retail and consumer banking, establishing itself as a key player in consumer loans such as auto loans, housing loans, and personal loans. The bank has consistently expanded its branch network and ATM presence across the Philippines to better serve its growing customer base.

Today, PSBank operates from its headquarters in PSBank Tower, 777 Paseo de Roxas Corner Sedeño Street, Makati City, Philippines. As of recent reports, the bank maintains approximately 250 branches and over 550 ATMs nationwide, serving the banking needs of Filipinos across the country.

Corporate Profile

Philippine Savings Bank (PSBank) is a major thrift bank in the Philippines primarily engaged in savings and mortgage banking. As of 2023, it is the second-largest savings bank in the Philippines, with the bank primarily catering to the retail and consumer markets.

Ownership Structure:

PSBank is a subsidiary of the Metrobank Group, with Metropolitan Bank and Trust Company (Metrobank) holding approximately 88% of its shares. The bank is publicly listed on the Philippine Stock Exchange (PSE) under the ticker symbol "PSB".

Corporate Organization:

PSBank's operations are organized into four key business segments, each representing a strategic business unit that offers different products and serves different markets:

  • Consumer Banking
  • Corporate Banking
  • Branch Banking
  • Treasury

Partnerships and Affiliations:

PSBank has a 30% ownership stake in Sumisho Motor Finance Corporation, a joint venture with Sumitomo Corporation of Japan (which holds 50%) and GT Capital (which holds 20%). Sumisho provides motorcycle financing solutions in the Philippines.

Network and Reach:

As of December 31, 2023, PSBank operates 250 branches and maintains 556 automated teller machines (ATMs) nationwide, providing convenient banking services across the Philippines.

Regulatory Framework:

PSBank was granted a quasi-banking license in 1995, expanding its financial service offerings. The bank is also authorized to perform trust functions, a capability it received approval for in 1991.

Scope of Business/Operation

Philippine Savings Bank (PSBank) operates as a thrift bank with a wide range of financial products and services focused primarily on the consumer and retail banking sectors. The bank's scope of business encompasses:

Core Business Areas

  • Retail Banking Services: Deposit accounts, consumer loans, and various payment services for individual customers.
  • Consumer Lending: Specialization in auto loans, home loans, and personal loans for retail customers.
  • SME Banking: Financial solutions for small and medium enterprises, including business loans and cash management services.
  • Trust Services: Wealth management and investment solutions for high-value clients.
  • Digital Banking: Online and mobile banking platforms providing convenient access to banking services.

Market Focus

PSBank primarily serves:

  • Individual consumers from various income segments
  • Families seeking housing and automotive financing
  • Small and medium enterprises
  • Urban and semi-urban customers across the Philippines

Banking Licenses and Capabilities

PSBank operates under the following licenses and regulatory approvals:

  • Thrift banking license from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP)
  • Quasi-banking license (granted in 1995)
  • Trust banking license (granted in 1991)
  • Electronic banking authorization

Geographic Reach

PSBank maintains a nationwide presence through its extensive branch and ATM network, serving customers across major urban centers, provincial capitals, and developing areas throughout the Philippines. The bank's operational coverage extends to:

  • Metro Manila and Greater Manila Area
  • Luzon
  • Visayas
  • Mindanao

As a member of the Metrobank Group, PSBank leverages strategic synergies with its parent company and other affiliates to enhance its market positioning and service offerings while maintaining its distinct focus on consumer and retail banking.

Capital Structure and Adequacy

PSBank maintains a robust capital structure that supports its business operations while ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and providing security to depositors and investors.

Capital Structure Components

Paid-in Capital and Surplus

These constitute the bank's core capital base, providing a buffer against potential losses and supporting business expansion.

Bonds Payable

As of December 31, 2022, PSBank had bonds payable amounting to PHP 4.65 billion. These bonds were issued in February 2020 with a tenor of 3 years and an interest rate of 4.50% per annum, payable quarterly.

Bills Payable

The bank reported bills payable of PHP 1.19 billion, primarily from interbank loan borrowings.

Corporate Governance

Corporate Governance Manual

This manual serves as the bank's guide in implementing governance standards, including compliance with the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Circular No. 969 and the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Memorandum Circular No. 19 s2016. Financial Performance

PSBank has demonstrated strong financial performance over the years, with consistent growth in net income, total assets, loans, and deposits. Below are highlights from the bank's audited financial statements for the past six years.

PSBank Audited Financial Statements (2019–2021)

2019

PSBank reported a net income of ₱2.11 billion, reflecting a 3.3% increase from the previous year. The bank's total assets grew by 4.5% to ₱359.3 billion, driven by a 6.1% rise in loans and a 5.2% increase in deposits.

2020

In the face of the COVID-19 pandemic, PSBank achieved a net income of ₱2.27 billion, up 7.6% from 2019. Total assets expanded by 5.9% to ₱380.7 billion, with loans increasing by 5.3% and deposits by 6.4%.

2021

The bank's net income reached ₱2.75 billion, marking a 20.9% growth year-on-year. Total assets rose by 6.8% to ₱406.3 billion, supported by a 7.8% increase in loans and a 6.5% rise in deposits.

PSBank Audited Financial Statements (2022–2024)

2022

PSBank achieved a record net income of ₱3.68 billion, a 139% increase from the previous year. Total assets grew by 8.6% to ₱442.7 billion, with loans increasing by 10.2% and deposits by 9.4%.

2023

The bank reported a net income of ₱4.53 billion, up 23% from 2022. Total assets expanded by 9.3% to ₱483.3 billion, driven by a 12% growth in loans and a 10% rise in deposits.

2024

PSBank posted a net income of ₱5.30 billion, reflecting a 17% increase year-on-year. Total assets reached ₱520.7 billion, with loans growing by 14% and deposits by 11%.
